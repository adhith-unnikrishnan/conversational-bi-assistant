🧑‍💼 Bottom 3 stores by revenue growth year on year
💡 Weakest revenue growth 2024→2025: Stratford (+3.0%); 3 stores shown.
Generated SQL
SELECT store_name, SUM(CASE WHEN year=2024 THEN revenue_gbp END) AS revenue_2024, SUM(CASE WHEN year=2025 THEN revenue_gbp END) AS revenue_2025, (SUM(CASE WHEN year=2025 THEN revenue_gbp END)) / NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN year=2024 THEN revenue_gbp END), 0) - 1 AS revenue_change
FROM v_sales
WHERE year IN (2024, 2025)
GROUP BY store_name
ORDER BY revenue_change ASC NULLS LAST
LIMIT 3
